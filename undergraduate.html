College Admissions & Passion Projects

A new model of college admissions consulting based on strong mentor relationships, expert strategy guidance, and impactful projects — all in one place.

Our Approach

01

Profile Review & Planning

We guide a detailed discussion to understand each student's goals and pair you with the right mentor. Then, we proactively guide major decisions including course selection, extracurricular growth, and passion project development.

02

Activity List Development

More programs, extracurricular activities, and competitions are available than ever for high school students, making it difficult to determine which opportunities matter for college admissions. We help students find and excel in a few activities that matter, contributing to the identity and narrative guiding their application.

03

College List Formulation

We create a low-risk, high-reward college list, balancing prestigious schools with less competitive programs we respect. At the center of the process are honest discussions and insider information to determine where we think your student will be happiest.

04

Research & Project Mentorship

Projects are our specialty. Our mentors guide students through both the self-reflection and practical skills necessary to complete a project that stands out, whether that's by publishing research, building a business, scaling a social media presence, or connecting with leading academics.

05

Writing & Review Process

From guided brainstorming through revision, we give students the structure to produce unique and engaging essays. Then we send your full application to multiple expert counselors and former admissions officers in our network to guarantee quality.

06

Decision Support

We arrange tours and meetings with recent alumni to evaluate different college experiences and outcomes. We also help turn your spot on the waitlist into an acceptance.
