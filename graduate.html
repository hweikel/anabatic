Master’s & PhD Admissions

We have helped hundreds of applicants receive MA/MS acceptances and fully funded PhD offers at universities including Harvard, Oxford, Cambridge, UCL, NYU, Columbia, UIUC, UW, Georgetown, CU Boulder, UT Austin, UW Madison, and the University of Indiana.

Our Approach

We help with every stage of the application process including:

  • Application strategy and program research
  • Research profile development
  • Resume and CV
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • Personal Statement (PS)
  • Interview preparation

For shorter pieces of writing such as SOPs, CVs, and Research Proposals, we prefer to edit the document with you live over Google Meet and Google Docs so that we can discuss your strategy and find the best way to express your meaning. Our sessions integrate application strategy, brainstorming, and editing into one productive meeting.

To understand our application support process and learn how to prepare, check out this YouTube video from one of our former students, now at the Harvard Graduate School of Education.
