Program Development

We partner with schools, startups, & researchers to develop impactful educational experiences.

Case Studies

Project-Based Curriculum Development

At Andover Summer, one of the largest and longest-running summer programs in the US, we developed a 5-week accelerated curriculum combining research and debate skill development with a long-term creative nonfiction project. Students had the opportunity to meet with speakers from industry, academia, and local government before presenting their final projects and competing in an extended tournament. Our course earned outstanding reviews from a diverse cohort of international secondary students preparing to enter T-20 US universities.

International University Admissions Preparation

At King Saud University in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, we developed a 4-week intensive summer curriculum for 180 secondary students from MENAP preparing to enter T-20 universities in the US and UK. Our lecture series covered topics ranging from college application essays to western academic culture, all tailored to an audience of advanced STEM and medicine students.

Capacity Building Workshops

We lead immersive experiences for teachers and administrators on implementing current educational research — often bringing in academics from the Harvard Graduate School of Education — and navigating new dynamics like AI literacy.

Research Publications

We have worked with dozens of early and mid-career researchers to promote their work and publish in high impact journals. Many of our clients are now in early career roles at leading American or British institutions or tenured abroad.
